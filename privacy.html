Costframe Tools – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Who uses it. Costframe Tools is used only by the owner of the Costframe YouTube channel, for that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses. With the owner's permission, it reads the channel's own YouTube data through Google's YouTube Data, Analytics and Reporting APIs, with read-only access: video titles and status, view and engagement counts, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate.

How data is used. Only to produce performance reports for the channel owner. Reports may be generated with the help of an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) working on the owner's behalf. Data is not used to train AI models.

Storage. Access credentials are stored in the owner's computer keychain. Reports are kept by the owner. Nothing is stored on any other server by this tool.

Sharing. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
